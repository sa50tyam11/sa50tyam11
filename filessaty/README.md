## 🧬 About Me

- 🚀 Founder of **[SENO Studio](https://senostudio.in)** — a web design & development agency in Muzaffarpur, Bihar, building sites, Discord bots, and custom software for Indian & international clients.
- 🧩 Building **Redwork**, a sister brand for smaller-ticket work (Discord bots, student projects, small business sites).
- 🎓 BCA student at Nitishwar Mahavidyalaya (BRABU), balancing coursework with running the studio.
- 🥋 MMA / martial-arts enthusiast, anime nerd — the name **SENO** itself comes from the sine wave (seno), a nod to signal & current.
- 🌱 Co-founded **[GrindLab.tech](https://grindlab.tech)**, a 100+ member engineering community.
- ⚡ Currently exploring AI automation & agentic coding workflows for client delivery.
